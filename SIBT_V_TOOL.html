    How it works

    1. Upload your photos and audios — every audio becomes one video section.
    2. Match a photo to each audio. A photo is never used on two audios at once — picking one swaps it automatically.
    3. Repeater loops the audio inside the video: a 7-minute audio at 7× becomes a 49-minute video with the same picture.
    4. Create All renders every video on this device (nothing is uploaded anywhere), then Download All saves them.
    SIBT V TOOL · Videos are rendered locally in your browser at YouTube size (1280×720 HD by default).
